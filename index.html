ABOUT THIS EXPLORATION

A city of possibilities.
Not a city of answers.

Common Ground imagines how people and robots might share a city in 2040. Each place begins with an ordinary human activity: eating, learning, moving, growing, caring, or simply being together.

The map is an invitation to look closer. Select a place, move through three moments, and take a question back into the city.

Find your own way

Mouse: drag and scroll. Touch: drag and pinch. Keyboard: Tab to a place and press Enter. On the map, use arrow keys, + / −, and Home. Escape returns to the atlas.

Motion can be paused. Reduced-motion preferences are respected. Your explored places are remembered only for this browser tab's session.
