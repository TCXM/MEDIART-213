## Future Telling illustrations

The following project-specific SVG scenes were created with coding assistance. They are speculative illustrations, not photographs, evidence of existing robot abilities, or measured research results.

- `shared-kitchen.svg`: neighbors prepare a meal with physical assistance from a robot.
- `workshop.svg`: a student adjusts fabric while a robot helps hold and fold it.
- `shared-evening.svg`: a courtyard gathering continues while a robot transports dishes.
- `neighborhood.svg`: shared spaces connected by walking paths; also the no-JavaScript fallback for the community scene.
- `community-connection.svg`: online coordination leads to an in-person workshop.

The animated neighborhood is drawn in p5.js by `community-sketch.js` at the repository root. p5.js version, source, and license are documented in [implementation notes](../../docs/implementation.md) and the [included license](../vendor/p5-LICENSE.txt).
